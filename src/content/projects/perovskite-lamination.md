## Project description

Perovskite semiconductors promise low-cost, high-efficiency solar cells, but bringing them to market requires manufacturing steps that are fast, repeatable and predictable at scale. This project, led by the University of Michigan with the University of California, San Diego, develops such processes together with predictive models that link process settings to product quality.

One of these processes laminates perovskite solar cells in seconds using rapid Joule heating, sealing the device as it bonds; heat transfer modeling guides how that step is designed.
