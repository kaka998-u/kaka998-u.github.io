## Project description

Measuring a battery's capacity today takes a full charge followed by a slow discharge of ten hours or more, so manufacturers oversize packs and decisions on reuse or recycling come slowly. This project combines physics-based modeling with machine learning to estimate state of health and remaining useful life from shallow charge–discharge data alone, resolving internal states such as SEI thickness and loss of active material rather than a single health number.

Faster, richer diagnosis supports right-sized battery manufacturing, onboard management that extends battery life, and quicker decisions on repurposing, reuse or recycling at end of life.
